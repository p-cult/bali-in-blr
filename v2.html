The programme

Four strands, eighteen days

Every strand pairs a Balinese practice with an Indian one. Nothing is a demonstration; everything is a collaboration built in the room.

01

Performance

Kecak, Balinese dance and Yakshagana, and shadow puppetry across both traditions — at Chowdiah, Jagriti, PCPA and Yuvapatha.

6 events
02

Workshops

Gamelan, Kecak, mask making and dance movement. Hands on, open to beginners, ticketed separately.

5 events
03

Talks & lecture demonstrations

An introduction to Balinese arts for students, faculty, researchers and anyone curious enough to come.

1 event
04

Exhibition

Masks, textiles and the ritual objects that carry these forms — alongside the food traditions they belong to.

Featuring

Dr. I Wayan Dibia

Padma Shri awardee & team

A renowned Balinese artist, scholar and choreographer known for connecting Balinese and Indian performing traditions. With over 150 choreographic works and seven books to his name, his work draws deeply from the Ramayana and Mahabharata.

He was awarded the Padma Shri by the Government of India in 2021 for his contributions to the arts and to cross-cultural dialogue.

A transdisciplinary ecosystem designed to inspire creativity, empower innovation, and celebrate India’s rich cultural, historical and scientific heritage.
