By event

"Clicked Book" is people the website sent on to BookMyShow or District. "Tickets sold" is what the team entered in the ticket sales sheet, from every channel.

How the ticket estimate works

  1. When someone arrives through one of our links, the website remembers which link for the rest of their visit.
  2. When they click a Book button, that counts as a "Clicked Book" for that link and that event.
  3. Each event's recorded ticket sales are shared out among the links in proportion to those clicks. If a link produced 30% of an event's Book clicks, it is credited with 30% of that event's sales.
  4. Sign-ups are counted only once the registration has actually been saved.

It is a fair estimate, not a receipt: BookMyShow and District never tell us which click bought what. Read the numbers by comparing links with each other.
