Bali in Bengaluru

Set in Grvtrn mn, the licensed face — this is the real font file, not a stand-in. Copper on charcoal, with the palette sampled from the Design Language board rather than guessed from its title.

What changed since the first pass

I had taken the colours from the board's description — Pantone 871 C, rose smoke — and proposed a muted gold. Sampling the ten pins directly gave a different palette: black and charcoal, oxblood and deep maroon, cream, with a warm metallic on top. Copper takes that metallic position, and unlike the gold it survives as text: 5.5 against charcoal, where saffron was decoration only.

Display — Grvtrn mn, licensed

Kecak at the amphitheatre

One weight only, so hierarchy comes from size, case and colour — never from a bold that does not exist. It has no rupee glyph, so ticket prices are set in the body face on purpose.

Body — Instrument Sans

Kecak, the vocal chant of Bali, is a complex form of performing arts integrating three main elements: vocal chanting, dance, and storytelling.

Sans throughout, as you asked. Quiet on purpose: the character is in the display face and the pattern.

Pattern

Drawn in CSS and SVG — scales to any collateral, costs nothing to load.

Both grounds, real content

An actual event from the calendar and the actual consent sentence — the charcoal one is what the site now looks like; the cream one is what it would become.
