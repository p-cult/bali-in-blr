The programme

Eighteen days of performances, learning & making

Performances

Kecak Performance

Performed by master artists, Kecak is one of Bali's most iconic performance traditions, rooted in ritual trance chants. A powerful chorus of men creates its signature “cak, cak, cak” rhythm, as dancers bring the stories to life through Balinese movement.

Balinese Dance × Yakshagana

The vocal rhythm and choral structures of Balinese dance meet the percussive, movement-based vocabulary of Yakshagana, exploring shared stories.

Shadow Puppetry

Collaborative puppetry performances by master artists of Indonesia and India.

Workshops

Shadow Puppetry Workshop

An introduction to Balinese and Indian shadow traditions, techniques of light, material, movement, and narration. Participants develop short sequences, and selected performers appear alongside master artists in a live presentation.

Mask-Making Workshop

Exploring Balinese mask traditions with symbolism, facial geometry, colour, and expression. Hands-on creation of masks using traditional and accessible materials, exhibited as part of the festival display.

Academic traditions

Dance & Movement Workshops

Traditional Balinese dance techniques, with a focus on body discipline, expression, and musicality.

Lecture–Demonstration Sessions

An introduction to Balinese arts and culture by Dr. Wayan Dibia for university students and faculty, practitioners, researchers, scholars, and heritage enthusiasts across Bengaluru.

Featuring

Dr. I Wayan Dibia

Padma Shri awardee & team

A renowned Balinese artist, scholar and choreographer known for connecting Balinese and Indian performing traditions. With over 150 choreographic works and seven books to his name, his work draws deeply from the Ramayana and Mahabharata.

He was awarded the Padma Shri by the Government of India in 2021 for his contributions to the arts and to cross-cultural dialogue.
